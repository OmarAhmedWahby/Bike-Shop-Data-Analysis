This project involved analyzing sales data from a global bike retailer to uncover key business insights and performance trends. The dataset was large and complex, requiring an advanced and carefully structured ETL process to clean, transform, and prepare the data for analysis. Using Power BI, I created interactive dashboards that highlighted seasonal revenue trends, customer purchasing behaviors, product performance, and regional sales distribution. The analysis helped identify peak sales months, top-performing product categories, high-revenue regions, and patterns in online purchasing vs. in-store

Here are some key insights from the analysis:

1. Revenue Peaks: We saw a significant revenue increase in July, October, November, and April, with July standing out as the month with the highest revenue.
2. Sales Drop: Sales experienced a notable decline in September, June, and March, with September seeing the largest drop.
3. 2013 – Record Year: The year 2013 emerged as the peak year for sales.
4. Highest AOV: July also saw the highest Average Order Value (AOV), contributing significantly to our performance.
5. Revenue Breakdown: $94M of our $109M in total sales comes from one key segment (Bikes) , representing roughly 86.2% of the revenue.
6. Online Purchases: 88% of our sales were made online, indicating the growing importance of e-commerce.
7. Top Region: North America led the way with $79M in sales, making up over 72% of the total revenue.
8. Order Distribution: On the other hand, Australia placed the most orders 6,800 out of 31,000 total orders
